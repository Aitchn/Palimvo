Vault home: a vault now opens on a front page with an activity wall (recent changes grouped by author, people and AIs can be filtered), the edit proposals waiting for review, and pinned notes. People with the vault-settings right can reorder the blocks, pin notes and choose a "start here" note, which guests see when it is public. The settings live in `.palimvo/home.json`.
