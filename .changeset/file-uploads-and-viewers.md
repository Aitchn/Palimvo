Upload files of any size: one request up to 64 MiB, resumable parts up to 4 GiB, and one-use upload links with ready-made bash and PowerShell commands. AI clients get `begin_upload`, `upload_part`, `finish_upload`, `cancel_upload` and `create_upload_link`. The web app shows a progress panel and opens pictures, sound, video, PDFs, JSON, CSV, YAML and text in place.
