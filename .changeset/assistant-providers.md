Assistant: the model provider layer for the built-in AI assistant. Anthropic and OpenAI-compatible endpoints (OpenAI, OpenRouter, Ollama, LM Studio, vLLM) are streamed in one common format, with cancelling, reasoning levels per model, and a scripted provider for tests.
